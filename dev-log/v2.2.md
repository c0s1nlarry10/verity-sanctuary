# v2.2 · Clocking Off

Pushed 2026-10-13 to https://github.com/c0s1nlarry10/verity-sanctuary (live on GitHub Pages).
Created by Liam R. Kinda helped with the idea: Manny V.

## 1. Goals for the version

- Give staff a home: a building you need before hiring, and a night routine.
- Let staff move freely like guests.
- Let players turn props and pens.
- Make pens look less copy-pasted.
- Keep the gold pile clear of buildings.

## 2. Staff House

- New object `staffhouse` (`kind: "service"`, `size: 2`, 400 coins). It unlocks at park level 4 with janitors, is Pro only (in `SIMPLE_OFF`), and you can only have one.
- Objects can now be bigger than one tile:
  - `objSize(t)` reads `OBJECTS[t].size`.
  - `rebuildGrids` marks every tile of the footprint.
  - `canPlaceObjectOf` checks the whole footprint, and the path rule and preview use the full size.
  - Objects are drawn with their feet at the bottom of the footprint and depth-sorted by their bottom row.
- Sprite: a 25x28 cottage (`makeStaffHouseSprite`) with a stepped roof, chimney, green sign, door, step, lantern and windows with flower boxes. Everything is on whole pixels, because the pixelate pass turns half-pixel frames into thick black blocks.
- Hiring is blocked until the house exists. The Staff card shows a "Staff House needed" row with a Build button that selects the tool. Once built, the card shows how many staff are home at night.
- The house can't be bulldozed while anyone is employed. Saves with staff but no house keep their staff, but can't hire more until one is built.
- The windows light up (with a soft glow) while any staff are inside.

## 3. Staff routine and movement

- Staff use the guests' movement system:
  - a smooth position, with walk legs checked by `guestPointOK` / `guestLineOK`
  - `roamGoal` for wandering, which now skips the "unseen pen" bonus for walkers without a `seen` list
- By day, janitors look for litter within 4 tiles and walk to it. Any walker steps onto a tile and sweeps its litter.
- At night (`staffNightPlan`), janitors head to the nearest litter while any is left. Everyone else heads home.
  - `pathRoute` finds the shortest route along path tiles (breadth-first).
  - `routeLegs` turns that route into straight walks: from where the walker stands, it heads for the furthest tile (up to 8 ahead) it can reach in a straight line, then repeats. So staff cut corners.
  - Night walks are 30% faster.
- Arriving on a path tile next to any side of the house sends a walker inside (hidden). Janitors come back out if new litter appears overnight. In the morning everyone steps out onto a door tile.
- Staff are drawn in the same depth order as guests and props, so they pass behind stands and the house.

## 4. Turning props

- Stands, the bench and the statue have `turns: true`, with four facings: front, right, back, and left (a mirrored right).
- `makeObjSprite(type, face)` draws:
  - side and back stands: the counter seen side on, or a plain back wall with a small sign of the food so the stands can still be told apart
  - the bench side on and from behind
  - the statue looking right, or the back of its head
- Facing is stored as `o.r` and drawn with `objSprite(t, r)`. Older saves face front.
- Controls:
  - R turns the next prop to place, or the placed prop under the mouse.
  - A Turn button appears in Shops & Decor while a turnable prop is selected.
  - Clicking the same placed prop twice within 5 seconds turns it.
- Turning props works any time of day. Props that look the same from every side say so instead of turning.

## 5. Pens

- `makeEncGround` and `makePenSprite` take a seed.
  - The big fixed set pieces are gone: trough, bush, curtain, lab machine, hazard stripes, Backrooms wall strip and light panel. Each theme keeps its floor, fence and food bowl.
  - Small details are scattered from `PEN_DETAILS`, by theme, avoiding the bowl, the pool ladder and the fence:
    - meadow: flowers, pebbles, rock, mushroom, clover, stick, puddle, dandelion, worn patch
    - pool: duck, leaf, float ring and ball in the water; towel, flip-flops and sunglasses on the grass (custom pools use water details only)
    - lab: bolts, oil stain, cable, beaker, hazard tape, vent, papers
    - stage: tape marks, rose, spotlight glow, speaker, star sticker, confetti
    - backrooms: damp stain, light panel, outlet, chair, footprints, crack
  - A small pen gets 4-5 details and a large one 6-7. Custom pens get one per few tiles, up to 8.
- Each new pen stores `seed`, and older pens use `id * 7919 + 1`. The build preview uses the next pen's seed, so it matches what gets built.
- Turning pens:
  - Square pens store `r`. The floor and details are drawn on their own layer, rotated, with the fence drawn on top.
  - Custom pens rotate their mask a quarter turn clockwise about their middle. They use `Math.trunc` offsets, so four turns land back where they started.
  - A custom turn is refused if any new tile isn't free or the pen would no longer touch a path. Animals inside move to new spots.
  - R turns the next square pen's layout while the Enclosure tool is active. Built pens have a Turn button on their card, or R on hover.
  - Turning a pen counts as building, so it's night only in Pro.
- The capacity signs drawn above pens were removed.

## 6. Gold pile clearance

- `nearVault(x, y)` covers the pile plus `VAULT_CLEARANCE = 1` tile. Paths, square and custom pens, and objects all use it, with the hint "Too close to the gold pile…".
- `canPlaceVault` also needs that ring free of paths, pens and objects. Upgrades grow in place even when older builds already sit beside the pile, before falling back to another spot.
- While a build tool is active, the zone is drawn as a red dashed box. Existing builds next to the pile are left alone.

## 8. Testing and release

- Headless-browser tests:
  - Vault zone: each build type blocked beside the pile; a path two tiles away allowed; the pile can't be moved next to the starting path.
  - Turning props: placing facing left, R, click twice, R on a lamp (no change), facing saved.
  - Every facing rendered and checked by eye. The bench side view was redrawn after the first pass read as a chair.
  - Pens: each theme at several seeds, sizes and turns, plus custom L-shapes, rendered and checked.
    - Turning a square pen and an L-shaped custom pen; a turn refused when the pen would lose its path.
    - Seeds and turns survive a save round-trip.
  - Staff:
    - hiring blocked without a house, second house refused, bulldoze refused while staff are employed
    - 60 seconds of daytime with four staff: never off the path, all moving, diagonal walking on a wide path, daytime litter swept
    - at night, remaining litter swept in about 11 seconds and everyone home by about 21 seconds
    - back out in the morning
  - Staff House sprite and lit windows checked up close.
  - Full smoke test:
    - a Pro park with all five themes, a custom pen, every prop type, the house and four staff
    - about 1.5 in-game days with guests, rendering throughout: no errors; guests peaked at 16, about 31,800 coins earned, all staff home late at night with no litter left
    - an older-style save (no seeds, facings or house) loads, keeps its staff, and blocks hiring until a house is built
  - Boot check: shows v2.2 with the new patch notes and no errors.
