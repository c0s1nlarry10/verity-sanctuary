# v2.1 · Simple & Pro

Pushed 2026-10-09 to https://github.com/c0s1nlarry10/verity-sanctuary (live on GitHub Pages).
Created by Liam R. Kinda helped with the idea: Manny V.

## 1. Goals for the version

- Make guests behave like real visitors: stay a while, then walk back out to their cars.
- Tell players what their guests think, so they know what to improve.
- Give pens room to breathe and stop variants looking packed in.
- Offer an easier way to play (Simple mode) alongside the full game (Pro mode).
- Make the game easier to learn: an empty start, hover explanations, tips and tidier panels.
- Fix the bugs players reported: the day report on the loading screen, evenings that looked like night, and dark specks for litter.

## 2. Technology

- Still plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step.
- A new script, `js/help.js` (tips bar and hover explanations), loads after `tutorial.js` and before `main.js`.
- Saves gained `mode` (simple or pro), `penVer` (pen size generation) and per-pen `k` (pen type). Old saves migrate on load without losing anything.

## 3. Guests

- Each guest stays a random time (about 20 seconds to 2 minutes, longer in bigger parks), then follows the paths back to the gate using a distance field from the gate, rebuilt at most twice a second.
- At the gate they become walkers again and retrace their exact route to their own car (or back down the street if they came on foot). A car leaves once its whole group is back.
- At 10 PM everyone hurries out. People still walking in turn round and go back the way they came, and the car park and pedestrian entrances close. Last entry is 9:30 PM.
- Guests stay inside their path tile, keep clear of grass, pens and props beside them, and stay back from tall stands below. Guests and props share one depth order, so a guest behind a stand is hidden by it.
- Mood falls 2 points per tile instead of 3, so guests in good parks see out their visit.

## 4. Guest feedback

- Guests can raise up to ten complaints: not much to see, nothing to eat, litter, crowding, dead-end paths, empty pens, hungry variants, a bare park, escaped variants and rain. Each one has a fix.
- A Guest Feedback card (Park tab) shows the share of guests who left happy, the top complaints with their percentages and fixes, and complaints overheard just now.
- The day report has a "What guests said" section. Rain always ranks last because players can't fix it.

## 5. Pens and events

- Pen sizes: Small 4x4, Medium 12x12, Large 20x20, still holding 4, 6 and 9 variants.
- Custom pens up to 50x50 tiles. Capacity, cost and appeal follow a smooth curve through the square sizes (2,500 tiles hold 14).
- Old Small pens grow to 4x4 where there's room. Older Medium and Large pens and custom pens keep their size and capacity.
- Each pen caches its own sprite, and the live preview of a pen being painted uses a one-slot cache, so even a 50x50 pen renders in about 8 ms a frame.
- "He belongs to the Backrooms" now stars Pirate Clark alone, dragging the variant towards a flickering Backrooms doorway. Each click makes him lose his grip.

## 6. Simple and Pro modes

- Every park picks a mode once, on its first launch, from a picker that can't be dismissed. Resetting the park lets the player choose again. The mode carries through prestige.
- Pro is the full game.
- Simple mode switches features off through the unlock system (`SIMPLE_OFF`) plus a few direct checks:
  - building at any time
  - no feeding, litter, escapes or staff
  - an unlimited gold pile
  - a car park that grows by itself at the Pro unlock levels
  - square pens only, with no fences, path types, remodelling or prestige
  - rain doesn't upset guests
- Simple mode also hides the cards and achievements for the features it removes. The tutorial skips the parking step and uses Simple wording.

## 7. New players

- New parks start empty, with 125 coins: exactly a Small pen plus the first egg, which always hatches a Verity. The free daily egg starts on day two.
- The tutorial has a "Build your first pen" step. Its first-run steps count as done if the player gets ahead of the tour.
- Skipping the tutorial on an empty park shows a hint about what to do first.

## 8. Interface

- The loading screen holds the game until the player enters, so a day can't end behind it. It has a tilted "Now with Simple mode!" sticker.
- Patch notes open by themselves the first time a returning player loads a new version.
- Hovering on anything for 5 seconds explains it, on the map (with Inspect) and in the menus. It can be turned off in Settings.
- The bar under the map cycles through 28 game tips. Tool instructions and warnings take over briefly.
- Every side panel card is collapsible and starts folded. Opened cards are remembered.
- A SIMPLE or PRO badge sits by the version number, and the Save tab shows the mode.

## 9. Fixes

- Lighting: open hours only get a gentle dusk (6 to 10 PM), and bad weather can't darken them to night levels. Night falls in the hour after closing and dawn starts at 5 AM. The "Night" label only shows when the park is closed.
- Litter is drawn at native pixel size in colour: soda cans, crisp packets, bottles, cups and napkins, each with a small shadow.
- `versions/archive.sh` now escapes "&" in titles.

## 10. Testing and release

  - modes, pen sizes and save migration

  - patch notes on new versions, lighting brightness at every hour, litter
  - hover explanations, the 5-second delay, the tips bar and collapsible cards
- Screenshots checked for the mode picker (desktop and phone), the loading screen, the Pirate Clark event, guests by stands, guests leaving at night, the day report and a 50x50 pen.
