# v2.1.1 · On the Path

Pushed 2026-10-09 to https://github.com/c0s1nlarry10/verity-sanctuary (live on GitHub Pages).
Created by Liam R. Kinda helped with the idea: Manny V.

## 1. Goals for the version

- Make sure everything guests pay for can be reached: pens and stands must touch a path.
- Offer a quick tutorial and a complete one.
- Make guests roam freely instead of marching tile by tile in lanes.
- Let the park run without anyone at the keyboard.

## 2. Building

- Square pens, custom pens and snack stands can only be placed touching a path (any edge, not diagonally). Decorations can go anywhere. The gold pile is not affected.
- The placement preview turns red when the rule isn't met, and the hint bar says why.
- Placement previews and post-hatch placing now follow `dayLocked()` rather than `isOpen()`, so Simple mode shows green and lets you place during the day.
- Pens and stands already built away from a path are left where they are.

## 3. Tutorials

- Starting or replaying the tutorial opens a picker: Basic tour (13 steps in Pro, 12 in Simple), Advanced tour (34 in Pro, 28 in Simple), or Skip.
- Steps are tagged by tour (`tour: "basic"` or `"advanced"`) and mode (`pro: true`, `only: "simple"`); text can be a function for tour-specific wording.
- The Advanced tour adds paths, pen sizes and themes, close-ups, feeding, managing pens, eggs, requests, guest feedback, guests, staff, the gold pile, parking, land, events, escapes, weather, the Lab, the Index, goals, New Sanctuary, daily rewards, saving, and the help features.
- The world clock waits while the picker is open. The waiting flag is tied to the picker actually being open, so closing it any other way can't leave the game thinking a tour is still being chosen.

## 4. Guests roam freely

- Each guest has a smooth position instead of a tile step. They pick a spot up to 4 tiles away and walk straight to it in any direction, and sometimes stop to look around.
- Choosing where to go:
  - Every path tile within reach is ranked: new places (versus the last 24 tiles), straight ahead, further off, and next to a pen or stand they haven't seen yet (4x).
  - The best few are checked for a clear straight walk, then one is picked by weight with plenty of randomness, so groups spread out.
- Safe positions:
  - Each path tile has a band where a guest's feet can be: the full tile where the path continues, kept clear of grass, pens and props beside it.
  - Corners that aren't path are kept clear too.
  - A walk is only allowed if every point along it is safe.
- When a straight walk isn't clear, the guest goes in legs:
  - first lining up level with the target (to slip past the narrow strip beside a stand or bench),
  - or via the middle of their tile (to turn corners).
- Leaving, guests head for the furthest spot towards the gate they can walk straight to, using the same distance field from the gate.
- Mood now falls over time (1.2 per second, more in rain). Litter and crowds count once per tile per guest, a guest's own group doesn't count as a crowd (four others are needed), and the dead-end complaint only comes from real dead-end tiles.
- New arrivals set off at slightly different times. Visits last 25 to 70 seconds, plus up to 60 for bigger parks.

## 5. Day report

- The end-of-day report closes itself after 30 seconds, with a countdown on its button, so the park can run on its own. Closing it sooner still works.

## 6. Fixes

- Skipping the tutorial no longer also says it was completed.

## 7. Testing and release

  - the path rule for pens, custom pens, stands and decorations
  - the Simple daytime preview
  - both tours clicked through to the end in both modes, with every step's target on screen
  - the auto-closing day report
- Earlier suites rerun:
  - guests, saves and modes
  - the empty start
  - patch notes, lighting and hover tips
  - collapsible cards and the 5-second hover delay
- A well-built test park: guests see about 5 of its 6 pens per visit and most leave happy.
