# v1.0 · Grand Opening

Pushed 2026-10-08 to https://github.com/c0s1nlarry10/verity-sanctuary (live on GitHub Pages).
Created by Liam R. Kinda helped with the idea: Manny V.

## 1. Concept

- A 2D pixel-art tycoon game in the browser, themed on the viral "Verity" meme (the Minecraft ARG by ThatMob and the song "It's Me, It's Verity").
- Genre: creature collector plus zoo tycoon, called **Verity Variant Sanctuary**. Hatch Verity variants, keep them in enclosures, and earn coins from visitors who come to see them.
- No story. All-ages: the Boobity variant is always shown behind a pixel mosaic.

## 2. Technology

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no build step, no install. Open `index.html` to play.
- Classic scripts loaded in order, sharing one global scope: `data.js`, `sprites.js`, `audio.js`, `game.js`, `systems.js`, `main.js`.
- Everything is drawn on an HTML canvas. All art is generated in code, so there are no image files.
- Sound and music are synthesised with the Web Audio API, so there are no audio files.
- Fonts: Press Start 2P (headings) and VT323 (body) from Google Fonts.

## 3. Game data (`data.js`)

- **27 variants** across six rarities (common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary, secret). Each has a colour, face, value, appeal, movement style and ability. Includes Verity, Lovity, Falsity, Cruelty, Obesity, Moggity (who mogs), Boobity (censored), Backrooms Verity, Steve-Verity and Pirate-Verity.
- **23 fusion recipes** for the Fusion Lab.
- **Individual variation**: 8 personality traits, 3 sizes, 5 markings, 9 accessories, and generated names. No two variants are identical.
- **Park**: a 24x16 tile map split into 4 land plots (Starter Meadow, East Woods, South Field, Far Corner).
- **Enclosures**: 3 sizes (3x3, 4x4, 5x5) and 5 themes (Meadow, Pool, Lab, Stage, Backrooms), each liked by certain variants.
- **3 path types** (dirt, stone, yellow brick) that change walking speed and appeal.
- **8 shops and decorations**: Verity Fries, Lovity Lemonade, Mog Burger, Flower Bed, Bench, Lamp Post, Fountain, Verity Statue.
- **3 staff types** (janitor, keeper, Verity mascot) and **3 egg types** (regular, golden, cursed).
- **Visitor types**: normal, kids, influencers and critics.
- **Weather**: sunny, cloudy, rain, storm.
- **Progression**: park levels 1 to 30 with an XP curve. 42 unlocks, hidden until reached. 16 goals and 19 achievements.

## 4. Art (`sprites.js`)

- Pixel art drawn procedurally at load: shaded variant spheres with faces, markings and accessories, plus visitors, staff, shops, trees, eggs, the gate and UI icons.
- A unique sprite is built and cached for each individual variant.
- Separate pixel-art UI icons for every panel.

## 5. Core game (`game.js`)

- Tile map with path painting, enclosure placement and a bulldozer that refunds half the cost.
- Hatching eggs, with a cracking animation and fanfare that scales with rarity.
- Placing variants into enclosures, plus a close-up "card" view to inspect, rename, pet and release each one.
- Visitors walk the paths, pay to view nearby enclosures, buy from stands and leave reviews.
- Income from variants on display, boosted by liked themes, abilities, traits and levels. Variants level up while on display.
- Fusion Lab with success chance, recipe book and lab upgrades.
- Variant Index showing discovered and undiscovered variants.
- Meme events, including "He belongs to the Backrooms": a tug-of-war between Steve and Pirate Clark that you win by clicking.
- Park XP and level-up unlocks.
- Saving: autosave every 10 seconds to localStorage, offline earnings (capped at 8 hours), and export/import of save codes.
- In-game confirmation dialogs (no browser pop-ups) and a park snapshot viewer.

## 6. Extra systems (`systems.js`)

- Purchasable land plots.
- Hunger and happiness care, with feeding and petting.
- Staff hiring and wages.
- Trash dropped by visitors.
- Visitor moods shown as thought bubbles.
- A day/night cycle with weather and night lighting.
- Visitor requests, escaping variants, special eggs, daily rewards with streaks, and prestige ("New Sanctuary") with Truth Shards.

## 7. Sound (`audio.js`)

- Chiptune sound effects for clicks, coins, building, hatching, fusing, level-ups and events.
- An original looping chiptune background track.

## 8. Interface (`index.html`, `style.css`)

- Pixel-styled UI: a top bar with park level and XP, coins, income per second, visitors, appeal, star rating and a sound toggle.
- A wooden park frame around the map, plus a hint bar and a goal strip.
- Side panel tabs: Park, Lab, Index, Goals, Save.
- Themed loading screen with a rolling Verity progress bar and the credits.
- Works on phones as well as desktop.

## 9. Testing and release

- Every system was tested with automated headless-browser scripts, alongside long simulated play sessions and screenshots at desktop and phone sizes.
- Published as a public GitHub repository with GitHub Pages hosting.
