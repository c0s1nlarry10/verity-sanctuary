# v2.0 · Bigger, Brighter, Busier

Pushed 2026-10-09 to https://github.com/c0s1nlarry10/verity-sanctuary (live on GitHub Pages).
Created by Liam R. Kinda helped with the idea: Manny V.

## 1. Goals for the version

- Turn the small v1.0 park into a large, explorable world with real opening hours.
- Give the whole game one consistent retro pixel-art style, with much more animation.
- Make progression last: steadily scaling prices, many more unlocks and a late game.
- Add the systems players asked for: custom pens, a bank, a car park, a tutorial and a way to send feedback.

## 2. Technology

- Still plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step. Open `index.html` to play.
- Scripts load in order: `data.js`, `sprites.js`, `audio.js`, `game.js`, `world.js`, `city.js`, `systems.js`, `admin.js`, `tutorial.js`, `main.js` (about 9,400 lines including HTML and CSS).
- **Rendering:** the world is drawn into a small low-resolution buffer at one art pixel per world unit, then scaled up by a whole number. Sprites, shadows, lights and effects all share one 16x16-per-tile pixel grid, and art is generated in code at 2x and reduced to that grid.
- **Camera:** pan with arrow keys, WASD or dragging; zoom with the scroll wheel, the +/- keys or pinch. Whole-number zoom steps, plus a whole-map view that always fills the screen.
- **Night lighting:** a light map. The scene is covered in darkness, each light cuts a banded pool out of it, then a warm or cool tint is added.

## 3. World

- 113x78 tiles, over 20x the v1.0 park. The original park sits in the middle, and older saves move across automatically.
- 42 plots of wild land in 11 biomes: oak, pine, birch, autumn, mushroom, rocky, flower, swamp, jungle, snowy forest and cherry blossom. The climate shifts across the map, and plots unlock from level 7 to level 30.
- Biomes blend into each other with no hard edges, using noise-warped sampling for both ground and trees.
- A city surrounds the sanctuary: a street grid of detailed buildings (brick, apartments, offices, glass towers, rooftop details), parks, plazas, car parks, traffic and lit windows at night. The camera only reaches its edge, and the rest fades into fog.
- A main road along the west side with side streets, zebra crossings, street lamps and pedestrians. Pedestrians cross the roads, walk into the city, and sometimes walk into the park as paying guests.
- A redesigned front gate (stone pillars, a timber beam, a pixel-font sign and lanterns) with a ticket booth.
- Minimap with click-to-jump.

## 4. Park hours and building

- The park is open 8 AM to 10 PM (8 real minutes) and closed at night (3 minutes), with buttons to open early or close early.
- Building, bulldozing, moving variants, buying land and upgrades all happen at night. During the day those controls are hidden. The Lab, Incubator, care and staff work all day.
- An end-of-day report at 10 PM shows visitors, revenue and expenses by category, profit, park XP, and every variant that levelled up.
- Custom-shaped pens: paint any connected shape (up to 12, 20 or 30 tiles). Price, capacity and appeal grow with the tile count.
- 7 fence styles and 5 themes, and pens can be remodelled.
- Locked items stay visible, greyed out, with the park level that unlocks them.

## 5. Guests, cars and parking

- Guests arrive by car. The car park has 10 levels, the last running from the top of the park to the bottom, for up to 900 guests at once.
- Cars choose a random free space, drive a central aisle in the bigger car parks, keep their distance, and back out the way they came. A barrier closes the car park at night.
- A zebra-crossing footpath takes people on foot from the road to the gate, so they never walk through traffic.
- Guests spread across the full width of the paths.

## 6. Money: the gold pile

- All coins are kept in a fenced gold pile near the entrance, guarded by two patrolling "Securities".
- The pile holds 30K at first. Each of 10 upgrades holds 5x more, and the vault itself grows from 3x3 to 8x8 tiles. Coins over the limit are lost, and reward messages say so.
- The gold fills the yard as a carpet of coins with several 3D coin heaps and gems.
- It is the only buildable that can be moved, at night. It relocates itself if the car park or an older save needs its spot.

## 7. Variants

- Every variant name ends in "-ity": Backrooms Verity became Liminality, Steve-Verity became Blockity, and Pirate-Verity became Piratity.
- 8 rounded body models, each with its own feature: classic, little arms, belly patch, curl and freckles, little feet, tufts and blush, spots, or ears and stripe. Every individual gets one at random. Accessories were retired.
- Bodies are a third bigger, and people, props and cars were rescaled to match.
- Jelly physics: squash on landing, stretch on take-off, leaning while moving and idle wiggles.
- Variants sleep from 10 PM to 8 AM with drifting Zs, and wake up with a stretch.
- Close-up emotes: each variant plays its own set based on its personality.
- Boobity wears a tank top instead of a censor bar, has blond hair in 8 styles (one per model), flirty emotes, and spring-based jiggle physics.

## 8. Progression and balance

- Tuned with a simulated player over 60-day playthroughs. The simulated player reaches level 29 in about 11 hours of play, fills about 31 pens, buys all 42 plots, and fills the full-height car park.
- Prices rise steadily instead of exponentially: eggs, pens, shops, decorations and staff.
- No staff limit. More pen slots as the park levels up (33 by level 30). A softer XP curve, and guests give more XP as the park grows.
- Gentler prestige (Truth Shards), a nightly litter sweep, and a cap on how much litter can hurt appeal.
- Timers for parked cars and lost pens run on game time.

## 9. Interface

- An animated loading screen: a night scene with a moon, shooting stars, hills, a gate, fireflies and a parade of all 27 variants doing their own tricks. It also shows the version number, rotating tips, and links to the credits, Instagram and feedback.
- Tagline: "a verity habitat tycoon".
- A skippable 12-step tutorial that points at each part of the screen and pauses the clock. It can be replayed from the Save tab.
- Collapsible patch notes for each version, with a Patch notes button in the top bar.
- Instagram (@jamesrobbizz) and Feedback (Google Form) buttons. A small feedback reminder every 10 minutes that can be closed, opted out of, or toggled in settings.
- An admin panel behind a passcode for testing: coins, levels, time, variants, land, events and weather.
- The mute button now silences music too.
- The map fills the window height, and the layout works on phones.

## 10. Testing and release

- Headless-browser test suites (over 300 checks) covering building, pens, land, parking, staff, saves and old-save migration, the gold pile, events, fusion recipes, prestige, the tutorial, emotes, cars, pedestrians and the admin panel.
- Multi-day play tests, 60-day balance simulations, and a performance test with 500 guests (under 1 ms per frame at normal zoom).
- A full mechanics review before launch, which fixed prestige balance, reward messages, vault placement, car park overlap and escape rules.
- Pushed to `main` and archived as `versions/v2.0`.
